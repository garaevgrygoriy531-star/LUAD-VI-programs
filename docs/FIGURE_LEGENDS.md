# Figure legends

Figure 1. VI-associated program changes across early LUAD histologic states. A, Histologic sequence schematic from normal lung through AAH, AIS, MIA, and invasive LUAD with the externally defined V1–V4 programs. Program-band intensity qualitatively summarizes stage-associated patterns and does not represent measured values; the sequence denotes pathologic categories rather than longitudinal sampling. B, V2–V4 scores across GSE308103 (normal, n=24; AAH, n=9; AIS, n=14; MIA, n=4; invasive LUAD, n=24; 75 specimens from 23 patients); squares show stage means and bars 95% patient-cluster bootstrap confidence intervals (5,000 replicates). C, AICc comparisons among gradual, invasive-step, and hinge mixed models; percentages indicate invasive-step preference across 1,000 patient-cluster bootstrap replicates. D, Distributions of AICc(gradual) minus AICc(invasive step) across 5,000 expression-, variance-, and detection-matched random gene sets; dashed lines denote observed values (empirical P = 2 × 10⁻⁴ for V2 and V3; P = 6 × 10⁻⁴ for V4). E, Step-minus-span comparison in GSE308103 and GSE319666. Open circles show cumulative earlier-state spans, squares the invasive-state increments, and filled circles the difference between those quantities; intervals are 95% patient-cluster bootstrap confidence intervals. V4 quantities are sign-aligned so that positive values denote movement in the aggressive direction.

Figure 2. Independent pathology replication of the invasive-state program pattern. A, Overlapping analytical patient sets in GSE319666: 40 patients overall, 38 in the primary contrast, 33 in the stage IA analysis, and 21 with paired earlier-state and invasive LUAD measurements. B, Invasive-LUAD-minus-earlier-state coefficients for V2–V4 in the primary analysis (129 specimens, 38 patients), with model-based 95% confidence intervals. C, AICc comparisons among gradual, invasive-step, and hinge models across all 177 specimens; percentages indicate invasive-step preference across 1,000 patient-cluster bootstrap replicates. D, Paired earlier-state and invasive LUAD scores in 21 patients; the lower strip shows mean within-patient differences with 95% confidence intervals from 1,000 paired-patient bootstrap resamples. E, Stage IA invasive-LUAD-minus-earlier-state coefficients in 92 specimens from 33 patients, with model-based 95% confidence intervals.

Figure 3. V1-adjusted stage associations and microscopic vascular invasion in the source VI cohort. A, Unadjusted and V1-adjusted invasive-state coefficients for V2–V4 in GSE308103 and GSE319666. Open circles show unadjusted estimates and filled squares V1-adjusted estimates; bars indicate 95% patient-cluster bootstrap confidence intervals. B, Associations of V2 and V3 with microscopic vascular invasion in GSE273528 (59 lesions from 58 patients; 17 VI-positive lesions). Points show odds ratios per 1-SD program increase from separate V1-adjusted Firth logistic models, with profile penalized-likelihood 95% confidence intervals. C, Cohort-grouped comparison of unadjusted and V1-adjusted invasive-state coefficients for V2–V4 in GSE308103 and GSE319666. Open circles denote unadjusted estimates and filled squares V1-adjusted estimates; gray segments connect model specifications for the same cohort and program. Right-hand values show the descriptive reduction in absolute coefficient magnitude, calculated as 100 × [1 − |βadjusted|/|βunadjusted|]. Adjusted estimates retained the direction of all six coefficients; confidence intervals are shown in (A).

Figure 4. Multimodal phenotypic convergence and stage-I outcome associations. A, Invasive-versus-AIS/MIA V2–V4 coefficients with 95% confidence intervals in GSE166720 and GSE282617. B, Solid-minus-pure-non-solid CT coefficients in GSE193725 (44 tumors from 43 patients). C, Paired regional program scores in seven GSE288479 tumors; open circles denote ground-glass regions, filled circles solid regions, and lines connect regions from the same tumor. D, Protein-set enrichment for V2–V4 in PXD046637 (65 processed samples); diamonds show normalized enrichment scores, with leave-one-measured-protein-out support summarized in the right-hand labels. V2, V3, and V4 enrichment used 19/37, 68/182, and 86/140 published program members, respectively; right-hand labels report sign-consistent leave-one-protein-out perturbations out of all measured-member deletions. These counts are sensitivity summaries rather than confidence intervals or independent biological repeats. E, Cohort-specific Cox hazard ratios per 1-SD score increase and random-effects pooled estimates for stage-I overall survival across GSE50081, GSE31210, and GSE30219 (323 patients, 78 deaths). Pooled hazard ratios were 1.58 for V2, 1.76 for V3, and 0.56 for V4; pooled intervals are Hartung–Knapp 95% confidence intervals, whereas cohort-specific Cox intervals retain their study-specific 95% definitions.

Figure 5. Multicompartment cellular distribution and external concordance. A, UMAP of 798,100 nuclei from 75 specimens and 23 patients, colored by broad lineage. The UMAP shows all major compartments; primary quantitative analyses were restricted to the 25 eligible fine cell types from epithelial, fibroblast/stromal, endothelial, and myeloid lineages that met patient-support criteria. B, V1-adjusted invasive-state differences in V2 and V3 from sample-by-lineage pseudobulk models with 95% confidence intervals. C, Patient-balanced invasive-LUAD-minus-earlier-state decomposition across epithelial, fibroblast/stromal, endothelial, and myeloid lineages. Stacked bars partition total contrasts into within-cell-type program-score differences, fine-cell-type composition, and covariance; black diamonds show direct total effects. The right-hand display shows component estimates with 95% patient-cluster bootstrap confidence intervals (5,000 replicates); circles, squares, and triangles identify within-cell-type, fine-cell-type composition, and covariance, respectively. D, V4 invasive-LUAD-minus-earlier-state total effects with bootstrap 95% confidence intervals. E, GSE189357 patient-level lineage differences comparing invasive adenocarcinoma with pooled AIS/MIA in 115,253 cells from nine treatment-naive patients. Background shading indicates the estimated difference; points and whiskers show effects and 95% bootstrap confidence intervals. Directional concordance was 7/8 for V2/V3 and 4/4 for inverse V4.
